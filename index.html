The Problem with Modern Day EV's

🔋

Limited Driving Range

Most EVs struggle with range anxiety, forcing drivers to plan every trip around charging station availability.

🔌

Heavy Reliance on External Charging

EVs are entirely dependent on charging infrastructure, leaving drivers stranded when stations are unavailable or occupied.

💸

High Costs

From purchase price to battery replacement, the total cost of ownership of EVs remains a barrier for most consumers.
